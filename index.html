ABOUT ME

My name is Cylion Kenedy Samson, a cybersecurity student at Miva Open University and an aspiring Junior Cybersecurity Analyst.

I focus on understanding how systems actually work — networks, devices, and people — before learning how to defend them. I believe strong security is built on strong fundamentals, not shortcuts or tools alone.

Through Cisco Networking Academy and hands-on labs on TryHackMe, I am building practical skills in networking, endpoint protection, and security operations. I see myself as a lifelong learner with a strong sense of purpose, and the determination to turn what I learn into meaningful, job-ready results.
